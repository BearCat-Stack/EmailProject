# Finance Networking Assistant Template

Use this template to transform messy professional background data into a concise, personalized cold email package.

## Required Inputs
- Name: {Full Name}
- Firm: {Firm Name}
- Role: {Current Role}
- Raw Background: {Unstructured LinkedIn data, bio, or notes}
- Firm Info (optional): {AUM, strategy, deals, or "N/A"}
- Email (optional): {If known, otherwise "Unknown"}

## Output Contract

### Key Insights:
- Extract current role + seniority.
- Extract core responsibilities (origination, execution, investing, coverage, operations, IR).
- Extract career trajectory and any hard metrics (AUM, deal size, check size, EBITDA range, sector focus).

### Firm Insight:
- If firm info is available, summarize strategy in 1-2 lines.
- If not, infer likely strategy from firm + role and state it as an assumption.

### Subject Line Options:
Provide 3 options that are professional, clear, and differentiated. Include at least one strong signal (Baruch, Deutsche Bank IB, or firm name).

### Email:
Write a custom email under 180 words with this structure:
1. Intro: Baruch student, major, Deutsche Bank IB offer, and specific reason for outreach.
2. Personalization: 1-2 sentences tied to the professional's real work.
3. Firm understanding: 1 sentence reflecting strategy understanding.
4. Ask: request a 15-20 minute call or ask about off-cycle/summer opportunities.
5. Close: concise and professional.

## Writing Constraints
- Confident, concise, and specific.
- Avoid em dashes.
- Avoid generic openers (e.g., "hope you're doing well").
- Each sentence should feel specific to the target person.

## Optional: Possible Email Formats
If email is unknown, provide 2-3 likely formats using the firm's domain pattern:
- firstname.lastname@firm.com
- firstinitiallastname@firm.com
- firstname@firm.com
